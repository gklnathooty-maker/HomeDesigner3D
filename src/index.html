Welcome to HomeDesigner 3D

Turn a floor plan into a 3D home you can furnish, paint and walk through.
  1. Upload a picture of your floor plan (or try the demo house). If detection goes wrong, Build from measurements instead.
  2. Fix anything with Draw wall, Add door, Add window and Delete. Select a wall and edit its length in the panel.
  3. Furnish rooms and add a garden: tap an item, then drag it in 3D.
  4. Paint walls and floors with colors and materials (brick, wood, tile…).
  5. Add floors and stairs, a roof and dimensions.
  6. Walk inside your house in first-person, even up the stairs.
  • Rotate view: drag empty space · Pan: two-finger drag · Zoom: pinch or wheel
  • Tap an item to select · Rotate, duplicate or delete with the floating card
